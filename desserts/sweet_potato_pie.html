mashed sweet potatoes, melted butter, granulated + light brown sugar, eggs, sweetened condensed milk, vanilla, cinnamon, nutmeg, optional ginger, Kroger Traditional Ready-To-Bake Pie Crust (2 ct)
Sauce & seasoning
Spice = 2 tsp cinnamon, ¾ tsp nutmeg (cut from 1½ tsp, which overpowers), optional ¼ tsp ginger. Melted butter whisked in (not creamed) so the filling doesn't puff, crack and sink.
Shortcut options
1 can (29 oz) cut sweet potatoes/yams, drained very well and mashed smooth, in place of boiling fresh.
User feedback
User prefers sweetened condensed milk over the original evaporated milk. Uses a Google Sheet for bulk batches (see below).
Future changes
Very sweet with condensed milk + 1¼ cups sugar; consider cutting granulated sugar to ¼–⅓ cup per pie (offered, not applied). Record sweetness and set after first bake with condensed milk.

Full recipe

Serves 8 · Prep 30 min · Bake 50–60 min · Cool 2 hrs + chill · ~$11–13 at Kroger

Ingredients (per pie)

  • 2 medium sweet potatoes (about 1½ lb), peeled, cut in 1" cubes, for 2 cups mashed
  • ⅔ cup butter, melted and cooled 5 min
  • ¾ cup granulated sugar
  • ½ cup light brown sugar, packed
  • 2 large eggs, room temp
  • ¾ cup sweetened condensed milk
  • 1½ tsp vanilla extract
  • 2 tsp ground cinnamon
  • ¾ tsp ground nutmeg
  • ¼ tsp ground ginger (optional)
  • ½ tsp salt (¼ tsp if using salted butter)
  • 1 Kroger Traditional Ready-To-Bake Pie Crust, 9-inch (2 ct / 14 oz frozen; second crust for extra filling)

Equipment: medium saucepan, electric mixer, large bowl, whisk, sheet pan, foil, wire rack, instant-read thermometer.

Directions

  1. Potatoes: Cover cubes with cold water by 1". Boil, then simmer uncovered on medium 13–15 min until a fork slides in easily. Drain, return to the pot on low 1–2 min, shaking, to dry. Beat with a mixer until very smooth; pull stringy fibers off the beaters. Measure 2 cups; cool 20–30 min.
  2. Oven: Heat to 425°F, rack lower-middle. Thaw crust 10–15 min; set it on a sheet pan.
  3. Filling: Whisk melted butter with both sugars until smooth. Whisk in eggs, then condensed milk, vanilla, spices and salt. Add sweet potatoes; beat on low about 1 min, just until smooth.
  4. Fill: Pour to ¼" below the rim. Extra filling goes into the second crust or greased ramekins (ramekins 25–30 min at 350°F).
  5. Bake: 15 min at 425°F, then 350°F for 35–45 min. Tent edges with foil if they brown early. Done when edges are set, the center 2" jiggles like Jell-O, and the center reads 175°F.
  6. Cool: On a rack 2 hrs, then refrigerate. Fully set after ~4 hrs.

Bulk baking: Mix 2–3 pies' worth at a time. Max 2 pies per rack on sheet pans; swap racks halfway through the 350°F stage.

Storage: Covered in the fridge up to 4 days. Freeze cooled whole pie, wrapped, up to 1 month; thaw overnight in the fridge.

Serving: Cold or room temp; warm a slice 10–15 sec in the microwave.
