Version A — Black Bean and Veggie Wrap (1 serving; 5 min)

¼ cup black beans (rinsed), ¼ cup frozen spinach (thawed 1 min in microwave, squeezed dry), ¼ red bell pepper in ⅛" strips, 1 tortilla (site listed whole wheat bread), ¼ tsp salt, ⅛ tsp pepper, 1 Tbsp Greek yogurt. Mix beans, spinach, pepper, seasoning; spread yogurt on wrap, fill, roll.

Nutrition: ~220 kcal, 10 g protein, 35 g carbs, 4 g fat, 8 g fiber.

Version B — Spinach and Black Bean Wrap (3 servings)

3 tortillas, 1 can (15 oz) black beans (drained, rinsed), 3 cups fresh spinach, ¾ cup shredded cheddar, 1 tsp chili powder, salt & pepper. Season beans; each tortilla gets 1 cup spinach, ⅓ of beans, ¼ cup cheese. Roll tight; serve or wrap for later.

Nutrition: ~350 kcal, 15 g protein, 45 g carbs, 12 g fat, 8 g fiber.
