Big-Batch Velveted Teriyaki Chicken Grain Bowls

Lunchsite import
Category
Lunch (meal prep, serves 5)
Main dish
Velveted chicken thighs and frozen stir-fry veg in doctored teriyaki over Success Rice, portioned into 5 bowls.
Side dishes
Built into the bowl.
Vegetable side
Frozen stir-fry vegetable blend (in the bowl).
Key ingredients
1.5 lb boneless skinless chicken thighs (1" pieces); velveting: 1 Tbsp soy (or low-sodium), 1 Tbsp cornstarch, 1 tsp oil, ½ tsp garlic powder, ½ tsp onion powder, ¼ tsp pepper, ⅛ tsp baking soda; 1 bag (12–16 oz) frozen stir-fry veg; 1 Tbsp oil for searing.
Sauce & seasoning
Double batch teriyaki — ¾ cup bottled teriyaki, 2 Tbsp soy, 1 Tbsp brown sugar, 1 tsp sesame oil, ½ tsp ground ginger (optional), ½ tsp garlic powder (or 1 clove); slurry kept separate: 1 Tbsp cornstarch + 2 Tbsp water.
Success Rice
  • Cooked rice needed: ~5 cups
  • Regular Success Rice bags: 2 (size up to family-size if needed)
  • Family-size Success Rice bags: 1–2
  • Rice prep note: fresh, plain. Check box yield, sizes vary.
Shortcut options
Rotisserie chicken (skip velveting, shred into sauce — faster but less juicy); bottled teriyaki doctored as above.

Full Recipe

  1. Toss chicken with velveting ingredients; marinate 15–20 min (or refrigerate a few hours).
  2. Mix sauce (slurry separate). Cook rice; keep warm.
  3. 1 Tbsp oil, skillet/wok, medium-high until shimmering. Sear chicken in batches 2–3 min until opaque and lightly golden. Remove.
  4. Same pan: frozen veg 4–5 min until tender-crisp.
  5. Return chicken, add sauce. Re-whisk slurry, add; simmer 2–3 min until thick and chicken hits 165°F.
  6. Cool 5–10 min, then divide rice, chicken, veg, sauce into 5 containers.

Containers: 5–6 × 32 oz microwave-safe with vented lids (glass is a good upgrade).

Storage: fridge 4–5 days.

Reheating: microwave covered 1.5–2 min, stir halfway; splash of water if rice is dry.

Cost: ~$14–18 total, ~$3/serving.
