Strawberry Peach Smoothie — full recipe (1 serving, ~16 oz)

Prep 3 min, blend 1 min. Mild, sweet-tart, thick enough for a straw without ice.

Ingredients:

  • ½ cup Kroger frozen sliced peaches (or 1 ripe fresh peach, sliced — then add 3–4 ice cubes)
  • ½ cup Kroger frozen strawberries
  • 1 Kroger plain Greek yogurt cup (5.3 oz)
  • ½ cup 2% milk (up to ¾ cup for thinner)
  • 1 tsp honey
  • ¼ tsp vanilla extract
  • Optional: 1 Tbsp chia seeds (+60 kcal, +4 g fiber; thickens it)
  • Optional: ½ banana for a creamier, sweeter version (+50 kcal)

Steps:

  1. Add milk first, then yogurt, honey, and vanilla. Liquid on the bottom keeps the blades from stalling on frozen fruit.
  2. Add frozen peaches and strawberries on top (and chia/banana if using).
  3. Blend on low 10 seconds to break up the fruit, then on high 30–60 seconds until fully smooth with no icy chunks.
  4. Too thick: add milk 2 Tbsp at a time. Too thin: add a few more frozen peach slices. Taste; add ½ tsp more honey only if the fruit is tart.
  5. Serve right away.

Prep-ahead option: the night before, put the yogurt, honey, vanilla, and chia in the blender cup and refrigerate; keep the fruit in the freezer. In the morning add milk and the frozen fruit, then blend. Adding frozen fruit the night before makes it thaw and go watery.

Storage: best fresh. Leftovers keep up to 24 hours in a sealed jar in the fridge; shake or re-blend (it will thin out and separate a little).

Kroger shopping: Kroger frozen sliced peaches (16 oz), Kroger frozen strawberries (16 oz), Kroger plain Greek yogurt cups — each bag of fruit makes ~4 smoothies.
