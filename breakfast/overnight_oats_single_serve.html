Single-Serve Overnight Oats (6 flavors)

Breakfastsite import
Category
Breakfast (1 serving each; 5 min prep, refrigerate overnight)
Main dish
Overnight oats in a jar, eaten cold (or microwaved 1 min).
Key ingredients
Old-fashioned oats, milk, chia seeds, honey or maple syrup, fruit, pinch of salt.
Sauce & seasoning
Method for all: combine oats, milk, chia, sweetener, spices, salt in a jar; add fruit; stir; cover; refrigerate overnight (8 hrs); stir and serve.
