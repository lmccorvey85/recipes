5-Day Overnight Oats Meal Prep

Breakfastsite import
Category
Breakfast (meal prep, serves 5)
Main dish
One base recipe with five flavor variations — make all five jars Sunday, eat cold all week.
Key ingredients (per jar base)
½ cup old-fashioned oats, ½ cup milk, ¼ cup vanilla or plain yogurt, 1 tsp chia seeds (optional, thickens).
Sauce & seasoning
Mix-ins by day:
  • Mon — Cinnamon brown sugar: 1 tsp cinnamon + 1 Tbsp brown sugar + ¼ tsp vanilla
  • Tue — PB banana: 1 Tbsp peanut butter + ½ banana, sliced
  • Wed — Apple cinnamon: ¼ cup diced apple + 1 tsp cinnamon + 1 tsp maple syrup
  • Thu — Chocolate PB: 1 Tbsp cocoa + 1 Tbsp peanut butter + 1 Tbsp honey
  • Fri — Berry: ¼ cup frozen mixed berries, thawed
Shortcut options
Single-serve peanut butter cups for Tue/Thu.

Save date
2026-11-17 (imported from recipe site)

Jars: 5 pint (16 oz) wide-mouth mason jars; 24 oz for apple cinnamon / PB banana.

Steps: Add oats, milk, yogurt to each jar, stir. Add mix-ins, stir. Lid and refrigerate at least 4 hrs (ideally overnight). Eat cold from the jar.

Storage: fridge up to 5 days.

Cost: ~$14–18 for 5 (~$3/serving), less with pantry staples.
