Chicken and Sausage Stir-Fry with Success Rice

Quick Weeknightsite importupgrade candidate
Category
Quick Weeknight (6 servings; prep 10 min, cook 18–23 min)
Main dish
Skillet stir fry of chicken, mild Italian sausage, bell pepper, onion, and frozen mixed veg in a soy-honey glaze.
Side dishes
Success Rice.
Vegetable side
Frozen mixed vegetables + bell pepper (in the dish).
Key ingredients
2.5 lb boneless chicken breast (1" cubes), 1 lb mild Italian sausage (½" rounds), 2 cups frozen mixed vegetables, 1 red bell pepper (¼" strips), ½ yellow onion (¼" dice), 2 garlic cloves, 2 Tbsp vegetable oil.
Sauce & seasoning
¼ cup soy sauce + 2 Tbsp honey + 1 tsp salt + ½ tsp pepper (split between chicken and sauce). Not yet doubled.
Success Rice
  • Cooked rice needed: ~6 cups
  • Regular Success Rice bags: ~3–4
  • Family-size Success Rice bags: 2
  • Rice prep note: fresh. Check box yield, sizes vary.
Shortcut options
Frozen mixed veg (or swap to frozen stir fry blend), Success Rice.

Full Recipe

  1. Cook rice (10 min), drain.
  2. 1 Tbsp oil, large skillet, medium-high; brown sausage 5–7 min. Remove.
  3. 1 Tbsp oil; chicken + ½ tsp salt + ¼ tsp pepper, brown 6–8 min.
  4. Add onion, garlic, bell pepper, frozen veg; cook ~5 min until tender.
  5. Return sausage. Mix soy, honey, remaining salt and pepper; pour over, toss, cook 2 min to thicken.
  6. Serve over rice; store leftovers in airtight containers.

Nutrition (per serving): ~460 kcal, 32 g protein, 45 g carbs, 16 g fat, 3 g fiber.
