Sunday Juicy Velveted Chicken Stir Fry with Success Rice and Honey-Butter Carrots

Standard Dinner
Category
Standard Dinner
Main dish
Juicy velveted chicken stir fry, cooked in a 14" carbon steel Yosukata wok.
Side dishes
Success Rice, honey-butter carrots.
Vegetable side
Honey-butter carrots, plus frozen stir fry vegetables in the main dish.
Key ingredients
Boneless skinless chicken thighs, frozen stir fry vegetables, Success Rice, carrots, soy sauce, oyster sauce, sesame oil, cornstarch, garlic powder, onion powder, black pepper, ginger, brown sugar, vinegar, butter, honey, optional green onions.
Sauce & seasoning
Chicken method — slice thin, velvet with soy sauce, cornstarch, oil, garlic powder, onion powder, black pepper, a small amount of baking soda, optional brown sugar. Cook in batches in a hot wok so it sears instead of steaming. Generous double sauce amount, but add ~75% first and adjust to taste.
  • User feedback: liked the meal, but it was a little too salty.
  • Required salt-reduction changes next time:
    • Use low-sodium soy sauce.
    • For 4 lb chicken: reduce marinade soy sauce to 3 Tbsp low-sodium soy sauce + 1 Tbsp water.
    • Reduce stir fry sauce soy sauce to ~2/3 cup low-sodium soy sauce.
    • Increase unsalted chicken broth or water to ~1 1/4 cups.
    • Reduce oyster sauce to 1/3 cup.
    • Don't add chicken bouillon powder unless sauce tastes flat after thickening.
    • Don't salt the Success Rice unless tasting at the end shows it needs it.
    • Reduce honey-butter carrot salt to 1/2 tsp.
Success Rice
For 8 servings, ~8 cups cooked rice — usually 4 regular Success Rice bags or 2 family-size bags. Check box yield, sizes vary.
Shortcut options
Frozen stir fry vegetable bags, Success Rice, bottled stir fry sauce doctored with broth/water, sesame oil, butter, garlic powder, ginger, small splash of vinegar.
Storage/reheating notes
Store rice separately from stir fry. Store chicken and vegetables with plenty of sauce. Reheat gently with a splash of water or broth, covered, to keep chicken juicy.
Future changes
Apply all salt-reduction changes above on next cook.
