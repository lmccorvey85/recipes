Southern Smothered Pork Chops with Onion Pan Gravy, Doctored Mashed Potatoes & Honey-Butter Green Beans

Standard Dinnersite import
Category
Standard Dinner (serves 8; active ~1 hr 20 min, total ~1 hr 30–40 min)
Main dish
Flour-dredged, seared bone-in pork chops smothered low and slow in caramelized onion gravy.
Side dishes
Doctored instant mashed potatoes, honey-butter green beans.
Vegetable side
Honey-butter green beans.
Key ingredients
8 bone-in pork chops (~7–8 oz each, 3.7–4 lb), Cajun/Southern blend, smoked paprika, flour, 3 large yellow onions, 2 brown gravy packets, low-sodium chicken broth, Worcestershire, brown sugar, instant potato flakes, milk/half-and-half, sour cream, cheddar (optional), frozen green beans, honey, butter.
Sauce & seasoning
Double sauce amount onion gravy — 4 Tbsp butter, 3 onions caramelized 20–25 min (medium → medium-low at 10 min, 1–2 Tbsp brown sugar in last 5 min), whisk in 2 gravy packets, then 5 cups broth in a slow stream; + 2 Tbsp Worcestershire, 1 tsp each garlic/onion powder, ½ tsp each pepper/smoked paprika. Chop rub: 1 Tbsp sea salt (go light), 1 tsp pepper, 1½ tsp each garlic powder/onion powder/smoked paprika, 2 Tbsp Cajun blend; dredge lightly in 1 cup flour.
Success Rice
Not used — mashed potatoes cover the starch.
Shortcut options
Gravy packets + caramelized onions (vs scratch roux from drippings); instant flakes doctored with butter, dairy, sour cream, cheese (vs boiled russets/Yukons); frozen/canned green beans (vs fresh blanched).

Full Recipe

Equipment: 12" cast iron (searing), Dutch oven (onions/gravy/smothering), large pot/skillet (green beans), saucepan (potatoes), meat thermometer, tongs, whisk.

Mashed potatoes: 4 cups low-sodium broth (or water) + 1 cup whole milk/half-and-half, 1 stick butter, 4–4½ cups potato flakes, ½ cup sour cream, 1 tsp each garlic/onion powder, salt & pepper, 1 cup cheddar (optional).

Green beans: 2 lb frozen cut green beans (or 3 × 14.5 oz cans, drained), 4 Tbsp butter, 3 Tbsp honey, 1 tsp garlic powder, salt & pepper.

Steps:

  1. 0:00–0:15 — Pat chops dry, season both sides, set up flour dredge. Slice onions. Measure everything.
  2. 0:15 — Start onions in Dutch oven (butter, medium) BEFORE searing chops. Heat cast iron medium-high with 1½ Tbsp oil.
  3. 0:17–0:32 — Dredge lightly, sear in 3 batches, 2–3 min/side until deep golden crust (not cooked through). Add oil between batches. Don't crowd.
  4. 0:35 — Onions deep golden and jammy → whisk in gravy packets, slowly whisk in broth, add seasonings, scrape fond, simmer medium.
  5. 0:42 — Nestle chops in gravy, spoon over, cover, low simmer 20–25 min.
  6. 0:50 — Bring broth + milk to gentle simmer for potatoes. 0:52 — off heat, stir in butter, then flakes gradually; fold in sour cream, seasonings, cheese. Cover.
  7. 0:55 — Steam frozen beans with 2–3 Tbsp water, covered, 6–8 min (canned: warm 3–4 min). Drain. 1:01 — medium-low, add butter + honey, toss 1–2 min, season, cover.
  8. 1:05–1:10 — Pull chops at 145°F (thickest part, away from bone). Rest 3–5 min tented. Reduce gravy 2–3 min if thin; splash broth if thick.
  9. 1:15 — Return chops to gravy and plate.

Storage: chops submerged in gravy (fridge 4 days, freeze 3 months); potatoes and beans separate, 4 days.

Reheating: chops + gravy covered over low 8–10 min to 165°F, spooning gravy over (microwave only at 50% power, submerged). Potatoes low with splash of milk. Beans medium-low with pat of butter.

Nutrition (per serving): ~700–750 kcal, 45–50 g protein, 35–40 g fat, 40–45 g carbs, ~950–1,150 mg sodium.

Cost: ~$42–50 total, ~$5.25–6.25/serving.
