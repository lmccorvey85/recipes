Cajun Shrimp & Sausage Skillet with Buttered Garlic Green Beans over Success Rice

Quick Weeknightsite import
Category
Quick Weeknight (meal prep, serves 6; active ~45 min, total ~55 min; mild-medium)
Main dish
Extra-saucy Cajun shrimp and andouille skillet with the trinity and Rotel.
Side dishes
Success Rice, buttered garlic green beans.
Vegetable side
Buttered garlic green beans.
Key ingredients
2¼ lb large shrimp (peeled/deveined, tails off), 1¼ lb andouille (½" coins, 2 packages), 1½ cups diced onion, 1½ cups diced green bell pepper, 1 cup + 2 Tbsp diced celery, 4–5 garlic cloves, 3 green onions; green beans: 3 cans (14.5 oz) drained or ~18 oz frozen, 3 Tbsp butter, 3 garlic cloves, scant ½ tsp Cajun seasoning, splash of broth.
Sauce & seasoning
Sauce doubled again on top of the usual double sauce (first batch ran thin and didn't coat the rice). 1 Tbsp Cajun seasoning, 1 Tbsp smoked paprika, 1½ tsp black pepper, 3 cups low-sodium chicken broth, 3 cans (10 oz) Rotel drained slightly, 1 Tbsp Worcestershire, hot sauce to taste, 9 Tbsp butter (3 for trinity, 6 to finish), 1½ Tbsp olive oil. Shrimp tossed with 1½ tsp Cajun seasoning. Build to ~75% of liquid, taste, then finish.
Success Rice
  • Cooked rice needed: ~6 cups
  • Regular Success Rice bags: ~3
  • Family-size Success Rice bags: 1 family-size + 1 regular
  • Rice prep note: fresh; if done early, keep covered off heat with a pat of butter. Check box yield, sizes vary.
Shortcut options
Frozen onion/pepper/celery blend (drain/pat; simmer sauce a bit longer), jarred garlic; store-bought Cajun blend (homemade lets you control sodium); frozen shrimp bag (cheaper).

Full Recipe

Equipment: 12"+ cast iron or heavy skillet (or Dutch oven), medium saucepan, rice pot/microwave, tongs, knife/board.

Steps:

  1. Prep: pat shrimp dry, toss with Cajun seasoning, refrigerate. Slice sausage; medium-dice trinity; mince garlic (separate portions). Drain beans and Rotel. Start rice.
  2. Sear sausage (6–7 min): oil, medium-high, single layer (2 batches if crowded), 2–3 min/side until deep golden. Remove.
  3. Trinity (5–6 min): medium, 3 Tbsp butter; onion/pepper/celery 4–5 min until onion translucent; garlic 30 sec.
  4. Sauce (5–6 min): stir in Cajun, smoked paprika, pepper; add broth, Rotel, Worcestershire; simmer medium 5–6 min until it coats a spoon.
  5. Shrimp (5–6 min): return sausage; nestle shrimp in sauce (2 batches if tight); 2 min, flip, 1–2 min until opaque and a loose "C" (tight "O" = overcooked), 145°F. Pull off heat immediately.
  6. Finish: stir in 6 Tbsp butter off heat until glossy; green onions.
  7. Green beans (start ~step 3): 3 Tbsp butter, medium; garlic 30 sec; beans + splash broth + Cajun; simmer uncovered 6–8 min until glazed.

Keep warm: skillet on low, lid ajar, ≤10 min — don't let shrimp sit on direct heat.

Storage: cool, portion into 6 containers with plenty of sauce; fridge 3–4 days. Don't freeze (shrimp texture suffers).

Reheating: covered pan medium-low with splash of broth just until warm — don't boil. Microwave 50% power, covered, stir halfway.

Nutrition (per serving): ~600–650 kcal, 38–40 g protein, 48–52 g carbs, 26–30 g fat, ~1,300–1,450 mg sodium.

Cost: ~$55–60 total, ~$9.25–10/serving.
