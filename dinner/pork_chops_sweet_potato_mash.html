Pork Chops with Sweet Potato Mash and Green Beans

Full Recipe

  1. Preheat oven to 375°F.
  2. Season chops with ½ tsp salt + ¼ tsp pepper. Oil in large skillet, medium; sear 4 min/side until golden.
  3. Move to baking sheet; bake ~15 min to 145°F. Rest 5 min.
  4. Meanwhile boil sweet potatoes 15 min until fork-tender; drain, mash with butter, milk, garlic powder, ¼ tsp salt, ⅛ tsp pepper.
  5. Steam green beans (or microwave with 2 Tbsp water) ~10 min until tender; season with ¼ tsp salt, ⅛ tsp pepper.
  6. Serve 1 chop, ¾ cup mash, ½ cup beans.

Storage: airtight, fridge up to 4 days.

Reheating (leftover remix): microwave 2–3 min stirring mash halfway, or oven 350°F 10–12 min.

Nutrition (per serving): 500 kcal, 30 g protein, 20 g fat, 50 g carbs, 8 g fiber.
