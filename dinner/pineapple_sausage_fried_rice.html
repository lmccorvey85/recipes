Golden Pineapple Butter-Glazed Sausage Fried Rice (Andouille + Italian Sausage)

Standard Dinnersite importnot yet cooked
Category
Standard Dinner (Quick Weeknight if rice is chilled ahead) — serves 4
Main dish
Wok pineapple fried rice with seared mild Italian sausage + smoked andouille, finished with a butter glaze. Sausage version of Better Golden Pineapple Butter-Washed Chicken Fried Rice.
Side dishes
Add one vegetable side — doctored green beans, asparagus, Brussels sprouts, or roasted broccoli. Avoid honey-butter carrots (carrots already in the rice).
Vegetable side
See above — not baked into the main dish.
Key ingredients
1 lb mild Italian sausage (casings off, pinched into ¾" pieces), 12–14 oz andouille (¼" half-moons), 6 cups cold cooked rice, 20 oz can pineapple chunks (drained, patted dry, ¼ cup juice reserved), ⅓ cup golden raisins (soaked 10 min), 3 eggs, 1 onion, optional green bell pepper, 2–3 garlic cloves, 1 cup frozen peas & carrots, 2–3 scallions, butter, lime (optional).
Sauce & seasoning
Double sauce amount — ¼ cup low-sodium soy, 2 Tbsp oyster sauce, ¼ cup reserved pineapple juice, 2 Tbsp water/unsalted broth, 1 Tbsp brown sugar, 2 tsp curry powder, 1 tsp each garlic/onion powder, ½ tsp each ground ginger/black pepper, 1 tsp sesame oil. Optional only if flat: 1 tsp fish sauce OR ¼ tsp Tony's (not both). Add ~75% first, taste. Sausages are salty — no bouillon, no added salt, no vinegar finish (lime only).

Shortcut options
Precooked Italian/chicken-Italian links; ½ cup bottled stir-fry sauce + ¼ cup pineapple juice + 1 tsp curry + 1 tsp garlic powder + 2 Tbsp water (skip oyster/fish sauce); or fried-rice seasoning packet + curry + pineapple juice + 1 Tbsp low-sodium soy; pre-chopped onion, jarred garlic.

Full Recipe

Equipment: 14" Yosukata carbon steel wok, sheet pan (chilling rice), meat thermometer, wok spatula, tongs, bowls.

Steps:

  1. Prep (~15 min): sausages cut, pineapple patted dry, raisins soaking, veg diced, sauce whisked, eggs beaten, cold rice broken up by hand.
  2. Preheat wok on high to light smoke.
  3. Sear pineapple dry, no oil, 2–3 min until edges caramelize. Remove.
  4. 1 Tbsp oil; sear Italian sausage in a single layer (2 batches if needed) 4–5 min to 160°F. Remove. Sear andouille 2–3 min for color only. Toss warm sausages with 1 Tbsp butter + 1 Tbsp sauce (glaze).
  5. 1 Tbsp butter, medium heat; soft-scramble eggs just until set. Remove.
  6. High heat, oil as needed; onion, pepper, garlic 1–2 min (don't burn garlic); add peas & carrots 1 min.
  7. Push veg aside; fry rice in two halves, pressing and tossing 1–2 min each.
  8. Drizzle ~75% of sauce around wok edges, toss, taste, add more gradually. Fish sauce/Tony's only now, only if flat.
  9. Fold back in sausages, pineapple, eggs, raisins, scallions; toss 1–2 min on high. Lime if using.

Keep warm: best served immediately; if holding, loosely covered on lowest heat ≤10 min (tight lid steams rice soft).

Storage: airtight, fridge 4 days; freezes 2 months (portion first).

Reheating: covered skillet/wok on medium with a splash of water, stirring; or microwave under damp paper towel, stir halfway.

Nutrition (main only, per serving): ~1,000 kcal, 45 g protein, 95 g carbs, 50 g fat, ~1,300–1,500 mg sodium.

Cost: ~$28–30 total, ~$7–7.50/serving.
