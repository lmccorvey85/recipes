BBQ Chicken Bake with Corn and Rice

Full Recipe

  1. Cook rice per package (boil 10 min).
  2. Preheat oven to 375°F.
  3. Oil in large skillet, medium heat. Season chicken with ½ tsp salt + ¼ tsp pepper; brown 5 min, stirring occasionally.
  4. In a 9x13, combine chicken, BBQ sauce, corn, onion, garlic powder, remaining ½ tsp salt + ¼ tsp pepper. Stir.
  5. Bake 30 min, stirring halfway. Top with cheese, bake 5 min more.
  6. Serve 1 cup chicken mix + ½ cup rice per portion.

Storage: airtight, fridge up to 4 days.

Reheating (leftover remix): 1 cup mix + ½ cup rice, microwave 2–3 min stirring halfway, or oven 350°F for 10 min.

Nutrition (per serving): 450 kcal, 28 g protein, 15 g fat, 50 g carbs, 3 g fiber, 18 g sugar.
