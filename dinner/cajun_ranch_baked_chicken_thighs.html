Cajun Ranch Baked Chicken Thighs with Success Rice, Roasted Potatoes & Doctored Glory Greens

Standard Dinner
Category
Standard Dinner
Main dish
Cajun ranch baked chicken thighs.
Side dishes
Success Rice, roasted cubed potatoes.
Vegetable side
Doctored Glory greens.
Key ingredients
Chicken thighs, ranch packet, Cajun seasoning, garlic powder, onion powder, black pepper, smoked paprika, optional brown sugar, oil, butter, mayo or sour cream, Success Rice, potatoes, Glory greens.
Sauce & seasoning
Original melted-butter-heavy seasoning mix clumped/hardened against cold chicken and was hard to spread.
  • Required fix next time: use a smoother spreadable seasoning paste instead — ranch packet, Cajun seasoning, garlic powder, onion powder, black pepper, smoked paprika, optional brown sugar, 1 Tbsp melted butter, 2 Tbsp oil, 1 Tbsp mayo or sour cream.
  • Prep note: pat chicken dry, let sit out 10–15 min while prepping, apply seasoning with a spoon, silicone brush, or gloved hand.
Success Rice
Always include full Success Rice bag guidance when this recipe is generated again.
Shortcut options
Ranch packet, pre-washed/cubed potatoes if available, canned Glory greens, Success Rice.
User feedback

Future changes
Keep the flavor profile the same, but make the seasoning easier to spread (see fix above).
