Smoked Sausage, Cabbage & Potato Stew with Cinnamon-Brown Sugar, Buttermilk Cornbread and Doctored Green Beans

Full Flavorsite import
Category
Full Flavor (slow cooker; ~8 servings)
Main dish
Thick, stew-consistency (not brothy) slow cooker smoked sausage, cabbage, and potato stew with a Southern-seasoned, cinnamon–brown sugar undertone.
Side dishes
Upgraded boxed buttermilk cornbread (Jiffy), doctored canned green beans (doubled).
Vegetable side
Doctored canned green beans (with bacon, onion, garlic, bouillon).
Key ingredients
Smoked sausage/kielbasa, green cabbage, Yukon gold potatoes (1.5" cubes), onion, celery, carrots, garlic, low-sodium chicken broth (2.5–3 cups), diced tomatoes, smoked paprika, thyme, bay leaf, Tony Chachere's, garlic powder, onion powder, brown sugar, cinnamon, cornstarch, bacon, canned green beans, Jiffy corn muffin mix, buttermilk, honey.
Sauce & seasoning
Revised from an under-seasoned soup version: Tony's 2 tsp, smoked paprika 1 Tbsp, thyme 1½ tsp, + 1 tsp garlic powder + 1 tsp onion powder, + 2 Tbsp brown sugar + ½ tsp cinnamon. No apple cider vinegar (disliked). Broth cut from 6 cups to 2.5–3 cups; stir in 2 Tbsp cornstarch + 2 Tbsp water slurry in the last 30–45 min and finish uncovered on HIGH to thicken. Salt/Tony's at the END, not the start. Optional: sear some cabbage in sausage drippings first for deeper flavor.
  • Cabbage overflow: if it won't all fit, add ~60% at the start, cook down 1.5–2 hrs LOW (~1 hr HIGH), then stir in the rest submerged. Count total time from the first batch.
Success Rice
Not used — potatoes + cornbread cover the starch. If added anyway: ~4 cups cooked for 8 servings (2 regular bags), plain/buttered, stored separately. Check box yield.
Shortcut options
Pre-chopped bagged cabbage (not coleslaw mix — too fine); jarred minced garlic; Jiffy mix doctored with buttermilk swap + honey + extra butter; canned green beans doctored with bacon, onion, garlic, bouillon.
User feedback
Cornbread and green beans great as-is, but green beans needed doubling for 8 servings.
Future changes
Next cook, confirm the cinnamon/brown sugar reads "warm," not "sweet" — if too sweet, cut to 1 Tbsp brown sugar / ¼ tsp cinnamon.
